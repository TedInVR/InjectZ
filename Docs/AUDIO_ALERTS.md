# Completion audio alerts

Choose **Inject Z > Audio Alerts…**. Click **Add Audio Files…** and choose recordings. M4A, MP3, WAV, AIFF and other macOS-supported audio can be imported. Preview and Stop Audio let you audition clips.

The top checkbox turns alerts on/off. Checked tracks play in random order, each once before the next round; the remaining order is retained between launches. At least two enabled tracks prevent consecutive repeats across rounds. New playback replaces previous playback.

One alert plays after each successful conversion, including multiple output formats and depth-map-only output. Errors and cancelled conversions do not announce. Showing an existing result manually does not announce.

Recordings are copied into `~/Library/Application Support/InjectZ/AudioAlerts`. The bundled melodies are installed here automatically; additional recordings can be imported.

## Included melodies

Builds include 28 M4A completion melodies created and supplied by Ted Whitten. They are installed automatically on first use; you can preview them, disable any of them, turn alerts off, or add your own recordings. Existing recordings and choices are preserved.
